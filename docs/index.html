Finish your routine.
Then your apps unlock.

Mingui is a daily habit checklist with a little buddy, and it keeps YouTube & co. locked until today's habits are done.

Google Play coming soon Mingui Root for rooted phones

Small habits, every day

Everything you need to build a routine, and a gentle nudge so you actually do it before the scrolling starts.

A checklist that sticks

Timers, weekday schedules, rest days, partial credit and streak freezes, so one off day doesn't wreck your streak.

Apps unlock when you're done

Pick the apps that eat your mornings. They stay locked until today's routine is ticked off.

A buddy on your screen

It wanders around your phone, cheers you on, climbs out of portals and collects hats as your streak grows.

Focus lock, if you want it

Pulls you back when you wander off, with three 5-minute breaks a day and an emergency exit.

Streaks & stats

A 12-week heatmap, your best streak, and the habits that need some love. Forgot to tick something? Fix yesterday.

English, Español, Català

Change language inside the app. Backups are a file you keep, and there's a home-screen widget too.

Your data stays on your phone

Mingui doesn't even have Android's internet permission, so it physically can't send anything anywhere. You can check: "full network access" isn't in its permission list.

  • No accounts or sign-up
  • No ads, analytics or trackers
  • No internet permission at all
  • Open source (GPL-3.0)

Two versions

Same app, same buddy. Pick the one that fits your phone; they can even live side by side.

Mingui Google Play

For every phone. When you open a blocked app, your checklist covers it. Uses Usage access and Display over other apps, and asks only when you turn on a feature that needs them.

Coming soon

Mingui Root GitHub

For rooted phones. Blocked apps disappear completely until you're done, and root grants the permissions for you.
