# Mingui privacy policy

*Effective 15 October 2026. Applies to Mingui and Mingui Root, by Abyss Studio.*

## The short version

Mingui collects nothing. It has no internet permission, so it can't send your data anywhere, and nothing you do in the app ever leaves your phone.

## What the app stores, and where

The following are saved only in the app's private storage on your device:

- the habits you set up, their schedules, timers and cues;
- which habits you ticked each day, and the times you ticked them;
- your blocked and allowed app lists, settings and your buddy's look.

No one else can read this data, including us. Uninstalling the app deletes it. If you use **Export backup**, the app writes a file to a place you choose. What happens to that file afterwards is up to you.

## What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. The app does not request Android's `INTERNET` permission, so it has no way to connect to anything.

## Permissions and what they're used for

- **Usage access** tells the app which app is open right now, only to compare it with your own block list. It is not recorded or shared.
- **Display over other apps** shows your checklist over apps you chose to block, and lets your buddy walk on screen.
- **Notifications** show a status line with a Pause button, and the reminders you schedule.
- **Run in background / battery** keeps the blocker running so Android doesn't close it.
- **Root access** (Mingui Root only) hides and unhides the apps you chose to block, and grants the app's own permissions.

Each permission is optional until you turn on a feature that needs it, and the app explains why before asking.

## Children

Mingui isn't directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If a future version ever added any network feature, it would be opt-in, and this page would say exactly what is sent.
